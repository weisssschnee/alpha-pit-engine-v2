# CN true1min Engine Mission

Build a point-in-time-safe A-share true-1-minute alpha research engine that can
express, evaluate, and compare static, temporal, event, state, fundamental,
chip, and plate/industry hypotheses without allowing validation, holdout, or
forward information to influence search.

The project succeeds by producing reproducible candidate identities, typed
field and operator contracts, matched controls, globally correct market-time
evaluation, bounded execution, and explicit evidence boundaries. Infrastructure
or development-positive results are not candidate promotion or OOS proof.

## Durable boundaries

- The complete field master owns field existence and status.
- The unified capability registry owns route and generator eligibility.
- PIT/observable-time authorities own historical availability semantics.
- Fixed split, candidate receipt, matched pair, and evaluator authorities own
  formal evaluation admission.
- Candidate-level validation, holdout, spent, sealed, and 2026 forward data
  cannot feed reward, admission, automatic scheduler or family credit, or
  persistent memory. A separately user-authorized report-only aggregate may
  freeze one offline bounded route budget; once used, it is spent route-design
  evidence and cannot support candidate promotion, optimizer feedback, or an
  adaptive within-tranche scheduler.
- Formal search, candidate promotion, and cross-sprint adaptive memory require
  separate explicit authorization.

## Current scope

The active line is CN true1min only. Legacy daily-search and proxy-only
promotion paths are outside the project mission. Heavy full-coordinate work
runs on 77o; local work is limited to source inspection, governance,
lightweight tests, and synthetic parity.
